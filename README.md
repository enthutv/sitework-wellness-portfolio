Sitework Wellness Store

AI-Powered Business Operations & Automation

Sitework Wellness Store is an AI-powered business operations platform designed to demonstrate how specialized digital employees can coordinate daily business activities through one connected system.

The platform brings together sales, customer support, order processing, inventory management, payment verification, fulfillment, financial monitoring, and quality control.

Instead of managing each business process separately, Sitework connects these activities through coordinated workflows, shared business information, and structured decision-making.

AI-Powered Operations Team

Eight specialized AI employee roles work together to manage business operations:

1. AI CEO — Oversees operations, monitors performance, and identifies areas for improvement.

2. Executive Secretary — Coordinates tasks, schedules follow-ups, and supports daily operations.

3. Sales & Customer Support — Handles customer inquiries, sales activities, and customer concerns.

4. Order Processing Officer — Manages orders, verifies order details, and coordinates processing.

5. Inventory Officer — Monitors stock availability, inventory movements, and product expiration dates.

6. Fulfillment & Delivery Officer — Coordinates order preparation, dispatch, and delivery tracking.

7. Finance Officer — Monitors payments, financial records, and reconciliation.

8. Quality & Compliance Officer — Reviews operational decisions, identifies exceptions, and supports quality standards.

Wellness AI Office

The platform includes an interactive virtual office where visitors can explore how the AI operations team coordinates work.

The office connects employee activities to actual workflow events within the application, allowing visitors to observe task assignments, handovers, reviews, and operational progress.
